Is the Category Review a sales call?

It's a working session. We look at your category before you join, walk our deck against it, and give you a cost range on the call. The Snapshot and the Category Plan are yours to keep either way.

If you need an NDA before sharing anything private, ask and we'll sign one first.

What does Pave cost?

Scope is written per client, so we don't publish a rate card. You get a realistic range on the call and the number in writing in your Category Plan.

How long do we have to commit?

Three months minimum, rolling after that. No 12-month lock-in.

Who actually does the work?

Named seniors. Media, creative, AI & automation, growth and measurement each have a named senior lead, and your Category Plan tells you who they are. No junior hand-off after signature.

What are AEO and GEO?

AEO (answer engine optimization) makes your brand the direct answer in Google's AI Overviews, featured snippets and voice assistants. GEO (generative engine optimization) gets you named and cited when buyers ask ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, Perplexity or Copilot for a recommendation.

SEO still matters, because it's how you rank in the results. AEO and GEO decide whether you're mentioned before anyone clicks. Your Visibility Snapshot shows where you stand on all three.

How fast will we see work in market?

Eight weeks from first look. Weeks 1–2 diagnose, weeks 3–4 design, and campaigns and agents go live in stages from week 5. After that we optimize weekly and review monthly.

We already have an agency. Do we have to switch everything?

No. Most clients start in one pillar and pull in the others once the first results land.

Bring your current agency's plan to the call and we'll tell you where we'd start and what we'd leave alone.

We're B2B with a long, committee-led sales cycle. How do you measure that?

Measurement is its own workstream with a named lead, reported weekly. We tie actions back to channel, creative and spend, score and route leads by intent, and put media, CRM and web in one view.

For a B2B solar company with committee-led cycles, moving spend to the decision stage, leading with ROI and matching forms to what sales needed cut cost per qualified lead by 41% and at least doubled sales-accepted leads.

Will AI agents publish anything without our sign-off?

Not without rules you approve. Campaign and content steps go live only with your sign-off. Customer-facing assistants run inside guardrails your team signs off on, with complex cases escalated to people.

Do you work outside the U.S.?

Yes. We're one global, remote senior team working across time zones.
