AEO · Answer engine optimization
The click is optional now.

Direct answers sit above the results, and many people never scroll past them. If the answer box names your competitor, your page-one ranking is working for them.

AEO is the work that puts your brand inside the answer itself.

  • Question mapping. The real questions buyers ask, by stage and intent.
  • Answer-first content. Pages that lead with the reply, then the evidence.
  • Structured data. Schema that tells engines exactly what each answer is.
  • AI Overviews and snippets. Formatting built to take the spot above the results.
  • Entity clarity. Unambiguous facts about your brand, people and products.
  • Answer monitoring. Which questions you own, and where you lost them.
